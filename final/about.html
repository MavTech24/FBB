About Food Basket Brewery

Pioneering premium beers crafted in the heart of Nigeria... quality, community and sustainability at every step.

Our Story

Founded in 2023, Food Basket Brewery began with a simple idea: craft world-class beverages using local ingredients and modern brewing techniques. From our first batch to today's expanding range, our journey blends tradition with innovation to deliver taste that unites communities.

We are driven by purpose — to create exceptional products while building meaningful partnerships with farmers, suppliers and the communities that welcome our brands.

Our Mission

Deliver outstanding beers and experiences while supporting local communities and sustainable growth.

Our Vision

To be Nigeria’s most trusted and loved brewery, known for quality, innovation and positive impact.

Crafting with Care, Building a Brighter Future

At Food Basket Brewery, we believe great beverages should do more than refresh — they should create positive impact. Through local sourcing, waste reduction and community programs, we are committed to responsible brewing and shared prosperity.

Our sustainability plans align with global goals, and we continually innovate to reduce waste, support farmers and champion responsible consumption.
